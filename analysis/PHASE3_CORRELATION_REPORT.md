### Interpretation Guide
- **Correlation Coefficient (r)**: Measures the strength and direction of a relationship between two variables.
    - **+1.0**: Perfect Positive Correlation (As X goes up, Y goes up).
    - **-1.0**: Perfect Negative Correlation (As X goes up, Y goes down).
    - **0.0**: No relationship at all.
- **Strength of r**:
    - **0.10 - 0.29**: Weak / Small effect.
    - **0.30 - 0.49**: Moderate / Medium effect.
    - **0.50 - 1.0**: Strong / Large effect.
- **Example**: If *Sattva* correlates **0.70** with *Conscientiousness*, it means students with high Sattva scores are very likely to be highly Conscientious.

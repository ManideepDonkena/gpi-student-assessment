### Interpretation Guide
- **T-Statistic / F-Statistic**: A score representing how different the groups are compared to the variation within them. Higher numbers usually mean bigger differences.
- **p-value**: The probability that the difference happened by random chance.
    - **p < 0.05**: **Statistically Significant**. There is a real difference between the groups (e.g., Males score differently than Females).
    - **p > 0.05**: **Not Significant (ns)**. Any difference is likely just noise or random chance.
- **Box Plot Interpretation**:
    - **Box**: The middle 50% of students.
    - **Line inside Box**: The Median (middle score).
    - **Whiskers**: The range of typical scores.
    - **Dots**: Outliers (unusual scores).
